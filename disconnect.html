Payment Insight is disconnected from QuickBooks

GDI Digital Solutions, LLC

Payment Insight no longer has access to this QuickBooks Online company. It will not send invoices, customers or payments to QuickBooks, or read from it, until someone connects it again.

What happens to your information

  • Nothing in QuickBooks is deleted. Customers, invoices, payments, accounts and products already sent stay in QuickBooks.
  • Nothing in Payment Insight is deleted. Your clients, notes, ledgers and invoices stay on your computer.
  • The access keys QuickBooks issued to Payment Insight are no longer valid.

Payment Insight also disconnects on its own whenever it is closed, a user signs out, or it sits unused for an hour — so seeing it disconnected in QuickBooks is normal.
