This Privacy Policy explains how Payment Insight (the "Software"), published by GDI Digital Solutions, LLC ("GDI", "we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Software and to its connection with QuickBooks Online.

In short: Payment Insight runs on your own Windows computer and keeps your data there. GDI does not receive, collect, store, sell or share your data. The Software connects to one outside service — Intuit QuickBooks Online — and only after you choose to connect it.

1. Who is responsible for the data

The business that installs and uses the Software (the "Licensee") decides what information is entered into it and is responsible for that information, including information about its borrowers. GDI does not have access to the Licensee's installation or its data.

2. Information the Software stores

The Software stores the information its users enter or import, which typically includes:

  • Borrower and client details: names, contact names, email addresses, telephone numbers, mailing addresses and property or collateral descriptions.
  • Loan information: amounts, balances, interest rates and terms, dates, ledger entries, invoices and payments.
  • Company details used on invoices, such as a company name, address and logo.
  • User accounts for people who sign in to the Software, with passwords stored only as salted one-way hashes.
  • Settings, including the connection details for QuickBooks Online (see section 4).

The Software does not collect payment card numbers, bank account credentials, government identification numbers or information from children.

3. Where the information is kept

  • All information is kept in a database on the computer where the Software is installed (Microsoft SQL Server Express LocalDB), in the Windows user profile of the person who runs it. Backups are written to that computer, or to a location the Licensee chooses.
  • GDI has no copy. The Software does not send your data to GDI or to any server operated by GDI.
  • The database and its backups are ordinary files and are not encrypted by the Software. We strongly recommend turning on full-disk encryption (Windows BitLocker) on any computer running the Software, and protecting the Windows account with a strong password.

4. QuickBooks Online

If you choose to connect the Software to QuickBooks Online, it exchanges information directly between your computer and Intuit Inc.:

Sent to QuickBooksRead from QuickBooks
Customers (name, email, telephone), invoices and their line items, payments, and — when you use Set up accounts — accounts, products and journal entries. Customers, products and services, accounts and their balances, invoices and whether they have been paid, and the name of the connected company.
  • You sign in to Intuit in a window provided by Intuit. The Software never sees your Intuit password.
  • The access keys Intuit issues are encrypted with Windows Data Protection, tied to your Windows account on that computer, and cannot be read from another account or computer.
  • The connection ends when the Software is closed, when you sign out, and after an hour without activity. Disconnecting revokes the keys with Intuit.
  • Information in QuickBooks Online is handled by Intuit under Intuit's own privacy statement and terms, which GDI does not control.

5. What the Software does not do

  • It does not send email or text messages.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or usage reporting, and does not report errors or crashes to GDI.
  • It does not listen for network connections; nothing can connect to it from outside the computer.
  • It does not check for or download updates on its own. New versions are installed by the Licensee.

6. Components from Microsoft

The Software is built on Microsoft .NET and displays its screens with Microsoft Edge WebView2, and it stores data in Microsoft SQL Server Express LocalDB. These components are supplied and updated by Microsoft as part of Windows and may send diagnostic information to Microsoft according to your Windows settings and Microsoft's privacy statement. The Software does not send your loan or borrower data to Microsoft.

7. Sharing

GDI does not sell, rent or share information from the Software, because GDI does not receive it. Information leaves your computer only when you connect QuickBooks Online (section 4), or when you yourself print, export or copy it.

8. Retention and deletion

Information stays in the Software until a user deletes it or the Licensee removes the database and backups. Uninstalling the Software does not delete the database or backups, so that a reinstall does not lose the book; the Licensee can delete them from the computer. Information already sent to QuickBooks Online remains there until it is removed in QuickBooks.

9. Security

The Software requires users to sign in, supports separate user roles, stores passwords only as hashes, encrypts the QuickBooks access keys, and signs out after an hour of inactivity. The Licensee is responsible for the security of the computer, its Windows accounts, its backups and who is given access to the Software.

10. Requests about personal information

Because the information is held by the Licensee and not by GDI, a borrower or other person who wants to see, correct or delete information about them should contact the business that services their loan. GDI will help a Licensee respond to such a request on request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the Software's features change. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be described with the release notes of the version that introduces them.
