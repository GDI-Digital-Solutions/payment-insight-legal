Connect Payment Insight to QuickBooks

GDI Digital Solutions, LLC

Payment Insight connects to QuickBooks Online from the Windows computer where it is installed. Connecting or reconnecting is done inside the app, not on this page.

To connect or reconnect

  1. Open Payment Insight on your computer and sign in.
  2. In the header, choose the company you want to connect.
  3. Go to the QuickBooks tab and choose Connect to QuickBooks.
  4. Sign in to Intuit in the window that opens and choose the QuickBooks company.
  5. The QuickBooks tab shows Connected and the company name.

Reconnecting to the same QuickBooks company picks up where you left off: what was already synced, and each note's products, are remembered. Connecting a different QuickBooks company starts its sync record fresh.
