Payment Insight is a Windows desktop application. It is not used in a web browser — it runs on the computer where it is installed, and connects to QuickBooks Online from there.

To open it

  1. On the Windows computer where Payment Insight is installed, open Payment Insight from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.
  2. Sign in with your Payment Insight user name and password.
  3. To work with QuickBooks, go to the QuickBooks tab and choose Connect to QuickBooks.

Not installed yet? Payment Insight is installed by your company's administrator. For help, email support@gdidigitalsolutions.com.
